Journey templates: six built-in starter journeys (inbound lead, trial onboarding, webinar, win-back, satisfaction after a conversation, sunset unengaged) in nb, sv and en, plus org-saved templates with `{{param:key}}` placeholders. New tools `journeys_list_templates`, `journeys_get_template`, `journeys_instantiate_template` and `journeys_save_as_template`, and the Architect skill `skill://journeys/draft-journey-from-brief` that turns a plain-language brief into a validated, simulated draft.
