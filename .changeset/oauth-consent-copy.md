Make the OAuth consent screen say plainly what each permission grants, in English and Norwegian. Several descriptions understated or misstated their scope: Conversations write also sets up channels, Feedback is feedback about Munin sent to the Munin team, the Knowledge Base line claimed you could "edit search", and the unused Commerce write line promised changing orders although commerce is read-only. Jargon such as "scopes", "credentials", "tracker keys" and "rotate signing secrets" is replaced with plain wording, and module titles now name what they cover ("Connected systems", "Feedback to Munin"; in Norwegian "Tilkoblede systemer", "Kampanjer", "Nettbutikk"). Norwegian labels that used "Utgående" as the name of the Outreach module now say "Kampanje(r)"; technical "outbound" wording is unchanged.
